## Checkpoint required by 29 November

- Complete, review, integrate and verify **every planned Sprint 2 feature** against its contract. Keep the accepted Sprint 2 release on `main` identifiable even when the D2 candidate adds Sprint 3 work. A scope change requires actual approval and a recorded rationale.
- Specify contracts, acceptance criteria (AC) and Software Test Specifications (STS) for **all remaining Sprint 3 features**. Implement and execute tests for **at least one meaningful Sprint 3 workflow or rule** on the candidate. Identify its tested commit and achieved AC.
- Release an integrated `main` candidate combining Sprint 1, all Sprint 2 work and the implemented Sprint 3 work. Deploy it on GCP through the Lab 5 pipeline. Prove critical earlier behavior, authentication, backend permissions, ownership and persistence still work.
- List remaining features/AC, defects, verification, owners, dependencies, next actions and dated targets through **13 December**. Plan around the **1-11 December exam period**. By D2, the product should be substantially complete and remaining work should be bounded.

## Evidence to capture while developing

- Select **two meaningful Sprint 2 demonstrations**, **at least one implemented and tested Sprint 3 workflow or rule**, and **one short original Sprint 1 journey**. Caption each with feature/AC, role, starting conditions, steps, expected and actual outcomes and tested version. Capture persisted reads, a meaningful rule rejection and backend/API evidence of role or ownership denial.
- Embed **two actual automated test excerpts** with setup, assertions and executed results. Use one domain or cross-feature rule and one access restriction. Include meaningful TDD RED/GREEN, actual commands, commit IDs and readable build/test/CI logs with passing, failing and skipped counts.
- Embed **two Issue/PR chains** with branch, author/reviewer, substantive review, staging integration and release history, preserving IDs and dates. Selected examples support the full feature and coverage tables.
- The PDF contains the required documents and appendices. Open with member names/IDs, assigned theme, repository URL, immutable submitted version, deployed URL/version and a page-based Parts A-E navigation table. Include runtime dependencies, environment-variable names/examples, Docker/build/run/test, migration/seed and deployment steps. Use synthetic data and private safe-account handoff or a local seed. Keep the assessed deployment and repository available through grading.
